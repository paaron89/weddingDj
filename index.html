FAQ

Good to know

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book 9–14 months ahead. Summer Saturdays go first, so check your date as early as you can.

How does the deposit work?

A 30% deposit secures your date. The balance is due 30 days before the wedding. You can pay by card or bank transfer.

Can guests make song requests?

Yes. Guests can request songs on the night or ahead of time through a link you share. I'll play them when they suit the moment and will always stick to your do-not-play list.

Can we give you a do-not-play list?

Absolutely. Your planning portal has must-play, play-if-possible and do-not-play lists. Anything on the do-not-play list won't be played, even if a guest requests it.

What equipment do you bring?

Professional speakers sized for your venue, wireless microphones, a clean DJ booth, lighting, and a full backup system on site for every event.

Do you have insurance?

Yes. I carry public liability insurance and all equipment is PAT tested. Certificates are available for your venue on request.
